# Pokemon sleep schedules vs lore (2026-11-25)

Built from what I know of every main-series game's encounter tables with a clock (Gold/Silver/Crystal, Diamond/Pearl/Platinum, HeartGold/SoulSilver, Sun/Moon/Ultra, Legends: Arceus, Scarlet/Violet) and the Pokedex entries. Red/Blue/Yellow, Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, FireRed/LeafGreen and Black/White have no time of day. This was from memory, not scraped live, so the MODERATE ones are worth a double check.

Rule used for the new **Sleeps** column: a daytime Pokemon sleeps at Night; a night Pokemon sleeps in the Afternoon (or the Morning if your game shows it in the Afternoon); a no-preference one sleeps in a slot it never appears in the wild in your game (Night first). Change any cell in the CSV - Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Night.

## Schedules that don't line up with lore

- **Weedle** (day): Verdant Forest in bug trees includes Night - lore says it's a daytime Pokemon. SS/HGSS Routes 2/30/31: morning + day only; at night those slots are Hoothoot/Spinarak.
- **Poliwag** (night): Verdant Forest surfacing in water is Morning only - lore says it's active at night. MODERATE: on land (grass) GSC/HGSS Routes 30/31 give Poliwag at night only; fishing/surfing at any time.
- **Poliwhirl** (night): Verdant Forest surfacing in water is Morning only - lore says it's active at night. MODERATE: as Poliwag (land encounters at night).
- **Ledian** (night): Verdant Forest flying is Morning only - lore says it's active at night. Dex (Gold): flutters about at night when the stars flicker, scattering glowing powder - though Ledyba is a morning Pokemon.
- **Wooper** (night): Verdant Forest running about is Morning only - lore says it's active at night. GSC/HGSS Route 32 / Ruins: Wooper appears on land at NIGHT only; Dex: comes out of the water when it gets cold (night) to find food.
- **Quagsire** (night): Verdant Forest running about is Morning only - lore says it's active at night. As Wooper (GSC/HGSS land encounters at night).
- **Dustox** (night): Verdant Forest flying is Afternoon only - lore says it's active at night. Dex: instinctively drawn to light; swarms gather at the bright lights of cities at night (a moth).
- **Mothim** (night): Verdant Forest flying is Afternoon only - lore says it's active at night. Dex (Platinum): flutters around at night and steals honey from Combee's hive.
